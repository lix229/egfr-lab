# Rapid eGFR decline: draft

*Written by an earlier agent session. Not yet reviewed.*

## Methods
Creatinine results from sites A, B and C were cleaned with the egfr-qc protocol: dates parsed per site, values converted
to mg/dL, duplicate results removed. eGFR was computed with CKD-EPI 2021. We excluded the 52 patients
with a single creatinine result, since a decline can't be assessed from one value. A rapid decline is a fall in eGFR of
more than 30% between two results no more than 365 days apart.

## Result
37 of 1,204 patients (3.1%) had a rapid decline: 14 at site A,
12 at site B and 11 at site C.
